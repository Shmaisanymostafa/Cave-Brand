# README artwork

`brand-overview.png` (also published as the social card `assets/social/cave-brand-og.png`) is a documentation composition of the repository's original English and Arabic full-colour SVG lockups and palette values. Logos are rendered without redrawing, recolouring, cropping or changing their proportions, on the approved white background. It is rendered in Chromium from the unmodified SVGs and the colours in `assets/css/tokens.css`. An earlier version showed desaturated logos and swatches (teal rendered as `#65979e` instead of `#179d9f`); this version was re-rendered and the swatch colours were checked against the tokens.

Sources: `assets/logos/cave-logo-en-color-compact.svg`, `assets/logos/cave-logo-ar-color-compact.svg` and the palette in `assets/css/tokens.css`.

The Cave marks retain the existing proprietary brand terms in the kit's `LICENSE.txt` and the brand guide. This composition grants no additional rights. No runtime assets or licences are changed.
