Rules for the logo

Do

  • Use the files as supplied. The wordmark is custom lettering drawn as outlines.
  • Put the full-colour logo on white or paper only. Put the white logo on teal, navy or charcoal only.
  • Keep generous space around it. The brand sets no fixed clear space or minimum size, so ask before setting one.
  • The symbol alone is for avatars and icons.

Don't

  • Retype the name in a font, redraw the symbol or recolour a layer.
  • Add a shadow, gradient or outline, or stretch or rotate it.
  • Put the full-colour logo on a colour or a photograph.
  • Add the retired line "Software Solutions".
  • Use it so that it looks as if Cave endorses or made your product.
